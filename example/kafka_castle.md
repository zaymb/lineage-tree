> Example: the design lineage of "File Under Castle", a semester-long architecture studio
> project on Kafka's The Castle (University of Sydney, 2026). Five cohorts are the project's
> stages: Start, then the four assessments A01a, A01b, A02 and A03.
> Lineage order top to bottom is a spectrum of kinship.
> Photography and composite sits between Archive and Model because names in both cross into it.
>
> Render it with:
>     python3 lineage_tree.py example/kafka_castle.md
